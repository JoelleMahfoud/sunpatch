Privacy Policy

Effective date: 8 November 2026

This policy explains how Sunpatch Farm (the "App"), made by Joelle Mahfoud ("I", "me"), handles your information. In short: the App does not collect, store on a server, or share any personal information.

Information the App collects

None. The App has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising and no analytics or tracking. It doesn't ask for your name, email, contacts, location, photos, camera or microphone.

Information stored on your device

To save your game, the App keeps your farm progress and settings (such as music and sound on or off, and which tips you've already seen) on your device. This information never leaves your device through the App. It's removed when you delete the App. It may be included in your own iCloud or computer backups, which Apple handles under its own privacy policy.

In-app purchases

Optional purchases are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. I never see or receive your payment details or Apple Account information. The App only learns which items you own, so it can unlock them, and it remembers that on your device.

Third parties

The App doesn't share information with third parties, because it doesn't collect any. The App is distributed through Apple's App Store. Apple may give me aggregated, anonymous statistics (such as download numbers and crash reports from users who opted in to share them with developers) under Apple's own privacy policy.

Children

The App is suitable for all ages and doesn't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the App changes how information is handled, for example by adding optional ads, I'll update this page and the effective date before that version is released.
