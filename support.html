Getting started

How do I play?

Tap an empty plot to plant, tap a growing plant to water it, and tap a ripe crop to harvest it. Lilly works through your taps in order. Put the harvest in the crate, then tap the To Market sign and Lilly takes it to your stall. A short guided first day walks you through all of this the first time you play.

How do I zoom and move around the farm?

Pinch with two fingers to zoom, and drag to move around. The + and − buttons also zoom.

Why aren't shoppers buying from me?

Someone has to be at your stall (Lilly or a hired attendant), and the stall needs stock. Shoppers also compare your prices with Rosa's and Bert's stalls. Open Prices and check the "Shoppers see" column. "Pricey" or "Too dear" sends people elsewhere.

What do Farm Levels do?

Every sale counts towards your Farm Level. New crops, beds, upgrades, staff, sprinklers and the Grand Greenhouse unlock as you level up.

What are golden crops and crows?

From Level 4, crops sometimes ripen golden. Only Lilly can pick them, and only for about 45 seconds. From Level 5, crows peck at crops: tap the crow to send Lilly, or put up a scarecrow (Shop › Land) to protect a whole bed.

When is the Village Fair?

Every 7th day from Farm Level 4. Enter up to three golden crops for cash prizes, reputation and trophies.

Saves and devices

Is my progress saved?

Yes. The game saves automatically at the end of each day and when you leave the app. Progress is stored on your device only.

Can I move my farm to a new device?

Your save is part of your device's app data, so it moves with an iCloud or computer backup restored to the new device. There's no online account.

How do I start over?

Choose New Game on the title screen. This replaces your current farm.

Can I turn off the music or sounds?

Yes. Use the Music and Sounds buttons on the title screen.

Purchases and privacy

Is the game free?

Yes. The whole game, including the Grand Greenhouse, can be earned just by playing. Shop › Extras has optional one-time shortcuts: the Greenhouse Key (build the Greenhouse straight away), Golden Boots (Lilly walks faster) and a Giant Watering Can (holds more water). They work on every farm you start.

I bought something. How do I get it on a new device?

Open the Shop, go to the Extras tab and tap Restore purchases. Use the same Apple Account you bought it with.

I was charged but didn't get my item.

Close and reopen the game, then tap Restore purchases in Shop › Extras. If it's still missing, email me. For refunds, Apple handles all payments: request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Does the game collect my data?

No. See the Privacy Policy.
